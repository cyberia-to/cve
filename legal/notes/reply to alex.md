Dear Alex,

Thank you for the edits and for the disclosure draft. Ten of your points are now in the pack as you wrote them or as agreed — the list is above. Fourteen questions remain, grouped by document.

**On the buyout ([[ceo]] [§8](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/ceo#8-exit) and [[shareholders agreement]] Article 5)**

1. §8 now buys your whole holding at the company valuation at the removal date, with a floor of USD 40,000 per 1% held. Confirm this is the basis you want.

2. The valuation is fixed as the price per share in the most recent arm's-length third-party sale within the 12 moon cycles before removal, and failing one, an independent valuer agreed by the parties. Is that method acceptable, or do you want a different one named?

3. SHA Article 5 funds the buyout through the Company first (Pasal 37 buyback) and then the other shareholders pro rata. As drafted, that puts half of the payment on Dmitry Starodubtsev for shares he never sold. Which is intended — Company first, or the seller personally?

4. The option package in §6 is written on founders' shares, not on new issue. On removal, do option shares return to the founder who sold them, or are they part of the same valuation buyout?

**On [[entry payment side letter]]**

5. Does the repayment obligation trigger on any cessation of office, or only on removal by the shareholders?

6. Does the side letter govern the entry stake, or does §8 now cover it as part of the whole holding? One of the two documents has to say so.

7. Does Dmitry Starodubtsev sign the letter, or does it stay between the two of us?

**On [[director-disclosure-indemnity-agreement]]**

8. Article 5 carries no cap, no basket and no threshold, a 20% uplift on knowing non-disclosure, 12% compounded interest, and unlimited survival for title, tax and criminal matters. What cap is intended?

9. Article 4 extends the same indemnity to any bona fide buyer, lessee or contractor of the Land, so every future Holder gains a personal claim against both Owners. Is that intended, and for how long after each transaction?

10. Appendix C has 11 rows to be confirmed or corrected and countersigned. By when, who prepares the corrections, and does the Effective Date wait on them?

11. Parcel 13 — 45,000 m², no transaction documents, and the Company already holds a [[pkkpr|PKKPR]] over it. Cured before the Effective Date, or disclosed and carried?

12. The USD 5,000 verbal debt to the former partner is payable on receipt of your share payment. Out of the USD 40,000, or separately?

13. Appendix A leaves the shareholders' agreement question unanswered. Should the executed [[shareholders agreement]] be listed as a Disclosed Matter?

14. Appendix D says the Commissioner may consent to a mortgage over the Land. [[ceo]] [§5](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/ceo#5-land-and-credit-hard-limits) says the Director has no such power at all. Appendix D yields to the Governing Documents, so nothing turns on it legally, but the summary reads the other way and should be corrected.

Two smaller items: the current Director's term runs to about 04.01.2027 — does your appointment run to that date and then renew, or start a fresh full term? And BANI, Article 11.3 — one arbitrator or three?

Alisa

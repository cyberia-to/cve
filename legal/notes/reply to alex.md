# Reply to Alex — what changed, what is asked

Answer to the edits received from veritus1, and to the disclosure draft of 2026-08-23. Two lists: decisions already applied to the pack, and questions that stay open.

## Part 1 — decisions applied

| # | matter | what was proposed | what stands |

| 1 | Pre-emption window | 30 days | 3 days — [[articles of association]] Article 7(4) |

## Part 2 — questions

### On the buyout route

1. [[shareholders agreement]] Article 5 funds the [[ceo]] [§8](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/ceo#8-exit) buyout through the Company first (Pasal 37 buyback), then the other shareholders pro rata. [[ceo]] [§8](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/ceo#8-exit) and [[entry payment side letter]] return the shares to the seller personally. Which route governs? As Article 5 reads now, half of a USD 40,000 repayment falls on Dmitry Starodubtsev for shares he never sold.

2. The option package in [[ceo]] [§6](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/ceo#6-options-up-to-10-at-4m-sale-from-founders-only) is written on founders' shares, not on new issue. On removal, do option shares also return to the founder who sold them, on the same personal basis as the entry stake?

### On [[entry payment side letter]]

3. Repayment trigger — any cessation of office, or removal by the shareholders only?
4. Does this letter replace [[ceo]] [§8](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/ceo#8-exit) for the entry stake, or sit alongside it? One of the two documents must say so.
5. Does Dmitry Starodubtsev sign, or does the obligation stay between the two parties?

### On [[director-disclosure-indemnity-agreement]]

6. Article 5 — the indemnity carries no cap, no basket and no threshold, a 20% uplift on knowing non-disclosure, 12% compounded interest, and unlimited survival for title, tax and criminal matters. What cap, if any, is intended?

7. Article 4 extends the same indemnity to any bona fide buyer, lessee or contractor of the Land. Every future Holder then holds a personal claim against both Owners. Intended, and for how long after each transaction?

8. Appendix C — 11 rows to be confirmed or corrected and countersigned. By when, who prepares the corrections, and does the Effective Date wait on them?

9. Parcel 13 — 45,000 m², no transaction documents, and the Company already holds a [[pkkpr|PKKPR]] over it. Cured before the Effective Date, or disclosed and carried?

10. The USD 5,000 verbal debt to the former partner is payable on receipt of Alex's share payment. Paid out of the USD 40,000, or separately?

11. Appendix A leaves the shareholders' agreement question unanswered. Should the executed [[shareholders agreement]] be listed as a Disclosed Matter?

12. Appendix D says the Commissioner may consent to a mortgage over the Land. [[ceo]] [§5](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/ceo#5-land-and-credit-hard-limits) says the Director has no such power at all. Appendix D yields to the Governing Documents on conflict, so nothing turns on it legally, but the summary reads the other way and should be corrected.

13. The current Director's term runs to about 04.01.2027. Does the appointment run to that date and then renew, or start a fresh full term?

14. [[bani|BANI]] — one arbitrator or three (Article 11.3)?

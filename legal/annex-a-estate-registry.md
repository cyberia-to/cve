- **Parcel 15**: two 2023 civil suits at PN Singaraja, both voluntarily withdrawn, land-book block lifted 11.01.2024. No live claim, but disclose on grant per [[director-disclosure-indemnity-agreement]] Appendix C item 2.
- **Parcels 18 & 19**: paper AJB deeds are filed under the wrong parcel (18's own deed is in 19's folder and vice versa); titles themselves are registered and clean. Correct the physical filing before either is offered.
- **Parcel 12**: otherwise clean; only the PPKPR lapse above applies.

## 3. KBLI × business address — ⚠ pending

[9A.1](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/hak-sewa-deed#part-9a-licences-warranty-and-licence-event-regime) requires one row per KBLI × business address from the NIB in OSS RBA, stating KBLI code, *alamat usaha*, *skala usaha*, risk class, *perizinan berusaha* status and any PB-UMKU required. The Company's OSS licence PDFs are on file (16 documents) but the address and risk-class fields did not extract cleanly in this pass — several documents show "Judul KBLI: Lihat Lampiran" (see attachment), meaning the actual KBLI list sits in an appendix page not captured by a flat text read. Populate this table from the OSS RBA portal directly, or hand the 16 PDFs to whoever finalises this annex for manual transcription, before relying on this table for [9A.1]'s warranty. Do not treat the absence of this table as the warranty being unmet — it is a gap in this annex's drafting, not a statement about the Company's actual licence position.

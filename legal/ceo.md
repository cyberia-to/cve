# CEO — PT Cyber Valley Estate

> **Offer / agreement (one text).** Draft for discussion until signed.  
> No separate economics annex, no open-items sheet, no long legal novel.

**Company.** PT Cyber Valley Estate ([[pma|PMA]]).  
**Mandate.** CEO who grows a **network of cities in Indonesia**, starting from **~37 ha** on **Sanghyang** (Bali): **Bali North Star / Cyber Valley** — nature × technology; mountain tourism + quality of life as tech hub.

**Job in one line.** Capitalise the structure and **decentralise ownership** so **founders (DS + AV) residual ≈ 10%**, with blocks in Indonesian hands, foreign hands (Cyberia holding), and residual for founders + CEO — without wrecking valuation.

## 8. Exit

Shares held by the CEO are ordinary shares under the [[articles of association]] and the [[shareholders agreement]] — [[rofr|ROFR]] and tag-along apply to any sale, the same as for every shareholder.

| trigger | rule |
|---|---|
| **Removal from Director office** (by resolution of the shareholders) | Removal takes effect **immediately**. Bought back are only the shares the CEO **bought from the founders** — the entry stake and any exercised option shares alike (options under [§6](#6-options-up-to-10-at-4m-sale-from-founders-only)) — each valued at the **company valuation at the date of removal** (determined under [§18](#18-valuation-methodology)), with a floor of **USD 40,000 per 1% held**, being the price he paid for it. Shares transfer to the buyer in tranches matching the payment schedule in "Payment of the buyout" below, not on removal itself — see that row and, for the entry stake, the [[entry payment side letter]]. Cause of removal does not change the price. |
| **What stays with the CEO** | Ladder equity earned from closed steps under [§7](#7-kpi-ladder-ds-av-10-founders-company-1b) is **not** bought out — it stays his, as ordinary shares. Commissions and referral fees under [§17](#17-survival-after-departure) stay his on their own terms. Unexercised options lapse. |
| **The valuation** | Determined under [§18](#18-valuation-methodology). |
| **Payment amount & schedule** | A debt of the buyer, not a condition of removal. If the CEO agrees, the amount is fixed at **USD 40,000 per 1% held** without a valuation, and instalments start immediately on that agreement. Absent CEO agreement, the amount is determined under [§18](#18-valuation-methodology) — fixed in US dollars as of the removal date (the §18 valuation date) — and instalments start as soon as that amount is finalised. Either way the amount is paid in **12 equal moonly instalments**, and the bought-back shares transfer to the buyer in the same twelve tranches — each tranche consisting of one-twelfth of the total bought-back shares, rounded down, with the remainder carried to the final tranche — released against its matching instalment. Until each tranche transfers, the CEO remains the registered holder of, and keeps all rights attaching to, the shares not yet transferred. On the fast-track, the shares transfer in full against that single payment. |
| **Currency, default & security** | Each instalment is converted to IDR at [JISDOR](https://www.bi.go.id/en/statistik/informasi-kurs/jisdor/default.aspx) on its payment date, as [UU 7/2011](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/laws/uu-7-2011-mata-uang) requires — the dollar figure does not move, only the conversion does, so the CEO carries no rupiah devaluation risk. Late payment — any instalment not paid within **5 days of its due date**, automatically and with no notice required — accelerates the entire remaining balance, whether owed by the buyer under this section, by a Founder under the [[entry payment side letter]], or by a Founder as an option-share shortfall obligor. **Primary, secured layer**: a dedicated escrow held with an independent notary and/or a bank guarantee or standby LC in the CEO's favour, established by GMS resolution before the first instalment under this section falls due, at a threshold equal to the then-current guaranteed floor (USD 40,000 per 1% held, recalculated at each Annual Reference Valuation under [§18](#18-valuation-methodology)). **Funding**: while the escrow balance is below that threshold, the Company sweeps 1% (one percent) of each revenue payment received — excluding capital contributions, loan proceeds, and transfers between the Company's own accounts — into the escrow within 5 (five) business days of receipt, through the maker-and-authorizer mechanism required for every disbursement under [[shareholders agreement]] Article 6; the sweep resumes whenever the threshold rises at an Annual Reference Valuation, or whenever a payout draws the escrow back down, until it again meets the current threshold. The escrowed funds are held by the notary, not as an entry on the Company's own books, so their availability does not depend on the Company's own signatures or solvency. **Enforcement**: on presentation of either a BANI arbitral award or a default certificate the CEO signs alone — no counter-signature or further act by the Company required. **Secondary, unsecured covenant**: for any part of an accelerated balance not recovered from that instrument, the Company must apply, in order — (i) available unrestricted Company funds; (ii) the Company's buyback capacity under Pasal 37 of [UU 40/2007](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/laws/uu-40-2007-perseroan-terbatas), applied to acquire the relevant shares directly — ⚠ capped by that statute at 10% of the Company's issued capital in aggregate across all treasury holdings, so available only to the extent that ceiling is not already used elsewhere; any shortfall this layer cannot cover for that reason passes to (iii); (iii) the reserve maintained under [[shareholders agreement]] (reserve allocation) — ⚠ that reserve is the mandatory reserve under Pasal 70 of [UU 40/2007](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/laws/uu-40-2007-perseroan-terbatas); confirm with counsel that it may be applied to a share buyback rather than only to loss-absorption before relying on it; (iv) a loan secured against Company-owned land, arranged independently of any security given to other creditors. Payment by the Company from either layer subrogates it to the CEO's claim against the defaulting Founder for the amount paid. This entire mechanism — amount, schedule, currency conversion, share-transfer tranches, security and covenant — applies equally to the amount payable under the [[entry payment side letter]] on removal (its paragraph 3). |
| **CEO's own decision to leave** | No buyout. The CEO keeps the equity already his — ladder equity earned from closed steps, options per time earned, and the entry stake — and may keep the stake and continue to receive dividends, or offer the shares to the founders and other shareholders first, then to third parties, under the [[rofr|ROFR]] in the [[shareholders agreement]]. |

## 9. Commissions, salary & asset management

Cash economics of the role — one page, no separate annex. Survival on
departure per [§17](#17-survival-after-departure).

**"Management company"** — in [9.3](#9-3-asset-management) below, any entity CEO participates in
that delivers on-site management of the estate's assets. Fees earned by such a company under
the [[property management]] offer belong to that company on the offer's terms.

### 9.1 Land-deal closing commission

Only for deals CEO closes himself. CEO is entitled to participate in the
estate-wide [[leasehold referrals]] program on the same terms as any other
referrer — 10% of amounts the Company actually receives, never the contract
sum — and holds no exclusivity: the program stays open to everyone, with no
priority and no territory attached to the role. Payable on each receipt into
the strategic account ([§2](#2-dual-accounts)).

### 9.2 Investment-raise fee

| | |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | capital invested into the Company, sourced or introduced by CEO |
| **Fee** | **5%** of the amount actually invested and received by the Company, payable to CEO |
| **Scope** | cash fee on capital introduction only — does not touch the cap table or the [§7 KPI ladder](#7-kpi-ladder-ds-av-10-founders-company-1b) |
| **Payable** | on receipt of funds |

### 9.3 Asset management

CEO is entitled to participate in companies that manage the estate's assets —
on the same footing as any other bidder. Management companies are appointed by
open tender, with no exclusivity attached to the role; deep-infrastructure
tenders (energy, water, data, computing, robotics) follow the same rule. The
current offer — [[carrot house]], [[banya]], [[soft]], [[satoshi]] and
glamping at 30% of revenue actually received — is published at
[[property management]].

### 9.4 Monthly salary — revenue ladder

Salary follows cumulative revenue actually received by the Company since
CEO's appointment. A ladder, not a discretionary number:

| cumulative revenue since appointment | monthly salary |
|---|---|
| below $100k | — |
| $100k reached | **$2,000** |
| $500k reached | **$5,000** |
| $2M reached (~[[sinwood]] sales done) | **$10,000** |

| | |
|---|---|
| **Base** | revenue = amounts actually received by the Company — the same doctrine as every fee in this document |
| **Step** | the salary steps up in the month a milestone is crossed and never steps down |
| **Floor** | Company accounts must hold **no less than USD 10,000** after the salary is paid — reduced or skipped if paying in full would breach that floor |
| **Interaction with [§1](#1-role)** | this ladder is the number behind the "ops-proposal" fixed salary referenced there — with the ladder in place, no monthly proposal cycle is needed |
| **Payable** | from the operational account ([§2](#2-dual-accounts)), once per month |
| **Reporting** | operational-account actuals for the closed month, the milestone standing, and this salary — at the moonly shareholders meeting under [[shareholders agreement]] Article 4 Cadence |

### 9.5 Common mechanics

| | |
|---|---|
| **Currency** | whichever currency the funds are actually received in by the Company; payable on receipt |
| **Survival** | confirmed by [§17](#17-survival-after-departure) — payable in full for any deal signed before the departure date, or any deal actively being worked at the time of departure, regardless of when the money actually arrives |
| **Reporting** | listed monthly in the [§3 strategic-gate](#3-strategic-gate-monthly) pack for visibility; no commissioner approval needed to pay out — this is a contractual entitlement, not discretionary spend |

## 18. Valuation methodology

| | |
|---|---|
| **Priority** | (a) price per share in the most recent bona fide arm's-length sale to an unaffiliated third party within **12 moon cycles** before the removal date — excluding sales to related parties, nominal/non-market consideration, and anything caught by [§15](#15-anti-circumvention); (b) failing that, the most recent Annual Reference Valuation (below), if dated within 12 moon cycles of the removal date; (c) failing both, the independent valuation below |
| **Appointment** | CEO and Founders each appoint one licensed valuer (KJPP or internationally recognised equivalent) within 14 days of the removal date; each delivers within 30 days of appointment |
| **Reconciliation** | Figures within 10% of the higher → their average. Figures more than 10% apart → the two valuers jointly appoint a third within 7 days (who delivers within 30 days); final value = average of the two closest of the three figures |
| **Default** | Either party misses the 14-day appointment window → the other party's valuer is binding. First two valuers can't agree a third within 7 days → appointed by MAPPI, or failing that [[bani|BANI]] |
| **Basis** | Under (a), the per-share price from that sale applies as agreed between its parties — no adjustment. Under (b) or (c): 100% of Company equity, going-concern, no discount for minority interest or lack of marketability; disregards any act caught by [§15](#15-anti-circumvention); includes a KJPP appraisal of the Land at current market value as a required component. Valuation date under any of the three = date of the removal resolution |
| **Cost** | Borne by the Company, including the Annual Reference Valuation below |
| **Annual Reference Valuation** | Company obtains an independent KJPP equity valuation at least once every **12 moon cycles**, timed with the annual GMS under Article 17 of the [[articles of association]], at the Company's cost — a running reference for the priority order above and any other purpose requiring a Company valuation |

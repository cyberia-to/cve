# Entry payment — side letter between Voinova and Fedorov

Private agreement between two individuals · not a corporate document · not filed with any authority · draft

Companion to [[share sale entry]] (the notarial deed) and [[ceo]] [§8](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/ceo#8-exit). This letter records the payment actually made between the parties for the entry stake, and the undertaking to return it on exit.

## Operative text

> **SIDE LETTER — ENTRY PAYMENT AND REPAYMENT UNDERTAKING**
>
> This letter is made on __________ between:
>
> **ALISA VOINOVA**, Russian citizen, holder of Russian passport No. 757428208, residing in Russia (the "Seller"); and
>
> **OLEKSANDR FEDOROV**, Ukrainian citizen, holder of Ukrainian passport No. FY773280, residing in Ukraine (the "Buyer").
>
> **1. Receipt of payment.** The Seller acknowledges having received from the Buyer the sum of **USD 40,000 (forty thousand United States dollars)** as the consideration agreed between the parties personally for **80 (eighty) shares** in PT. CYBER VALLEY ESTATE, being 1% of the issued capital of 8,040 shares (the "Entry Shares"). This letter is a valid receipt for that sum.
>
> **2. Relationship to the notarial deed.** The number of Entry Shares is the number stated in the notarial deed — 80 (eighty) shares. This letter governs the price: the sum in paragraph 1 is the amount actually paid between the parties for those shares, and it is that sum, not the figure recited in the deed, that is repaid under paragraph 3.
>
> **3. Repayment undertaking.** If the Buyer is removed from the office of President Director by resolution of the shareholders, the Seller undertakes to pay to the Buyer an amount equal to the greater of (a) USD 40,000, and (b) the value of the Entry Shares calculated at the Company's valuation on the date of removal, determined as set out in [[ceo]] [§18](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/ceo#18-valuation-methodology), and the Buyer undertakes to transfer the Entry Shares back to the Seller. The Seller is the primary obligor for each instalment. If any instalment is not paid within 5 days of its due date, the entire unpaid remainder under this paragraph accelerates and becomes payable in full immediately, and the Company becomes liable for that full remainder as a backstop, automatically, without needing the Company's own signature or any notice of non-payment to the Seller — first against the escrow/guarantee instrument under [[ceo]] [§8](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/ceo#8-exit) ("Currency, default & security"), and for any part not recovered there, directly against the Company on the covenant terms of that same section. Payment by the Company under this paragraph, from either source, subrogates it to the Buyer's claim against the Seller for the amount paid. Timing for both acts is as set out in paragraph 4.
>
> **4. Timing.** The Entry Shares are re-transferred to the Seller in **12 tranches**, each consisting of one-twelfth of the Entry Shares (any rounding remainder carried to the final tranche), each tranche released against the matching instalment of the amount under paragraph 3 — on the same instalment schedule, currency-conversion mechanism and security as the buyout of option shares under [[ceo]] [§8](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/ceo#8-exit) ("Payment amount & schedule" and "Currency, default & security"). On the CEO-agreed USD 40,000 fast-track without a valuation, all Entry Shares transfer at once against that single payment. Where the Buyer resigns or otherwise leaves office of his own decision, this letter does not apply and he keeps the Entry Shares. Until each tranche is transferred, the Buyer remains the registered holder of, and keeps all rights attaching to (including dividends on), the not-yet-transferred Entry Shares.
>
> **5. No additional interest.** No interest accrues on the amount payable under paragraph 3, whether before or after the event described there, beyond the valuation-based calculation in paragraph 3 itself.
>
> **6. Currency.** The sum is denominated and repayable in United States dollars. Where payment is made in another currency, the conversion is at the [[jisdor|JISDOR]] rate on the date of payment.
>
> **7. Scope.** This letter covers the Entry Shares only. Shares acquired by the Buyer under the option package in [[ceo]] [§6](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/ceo#6-options-up-to-10-at-4m-sale-from-founders-only), and equity earned under the KPI ladder in [[ceo]] [§7](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/ceo#7-kpi-ladder-ds-av-10-founders-company-1b), are outside this letter and are governed by their own terms.
>
> **8. Confidentiality.** The parties keep the terms of this letter confidential, save where disclosure is required by law or by a competent authority.
>
> **9. Governing law and disputes.** This letter is governed by the laws of the Republic of Indonesia. Disputes are settled by good-faith negotiation and, failing agreement within 30 days, by arbitration under [[bani|BANI]] rules, seat Bali, in English.
>
> **10. Counterparts.** This letter may be signed in counterparts, each of which is an original, together forming one instrument.
>

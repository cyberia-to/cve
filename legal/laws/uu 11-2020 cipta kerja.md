# UU 11/2020 — Cipta Kerja / Job Creation

Undang-Undang Republik Indonesia Nomor 11 Tahun 2020 tentang Cipta Kerja — the omnibus law that amended a long list of statutes at once.

Source: [JDIH ATR/BPN](https://jdih.atrbpn.go.id/peraturan/detail/258/undang-undang-nomor-11-tahun-2020)

It appears in the pack twice, always as the amending instrument rather than the operative text.

| where | what it did |
|---|---|
| [[uu 2-2017 jasa konstruksi]] | amended the construction services law, including Pasal 30 on the [[sbu\|Sertifikat Badan Usaha]] |
| [[pp 18-2021 hak atas tanah]] | Pasal 142 and Pasal 185 letter b are the basis on which PP 18/2021 was made |

When citing either of those, cite the operative provision and note that it stands as amended by this law.

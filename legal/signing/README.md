# signing

- [[signing entry pack|pack 1 — entry of the president director]]
- [[signing land pack|pack 2 — land, sinwood-25]]
- [[signing reference|reference — held, unsigned]]

Two packs are executed, in that order. The order inside each pack is the
order of signature, set by what each instrument depends on — not by
alphabet and not by the numbering in [[dzin]], which is a catalogue.

## why the order is what it is

A document can be signed once the thing it rests on exists. That single
rule produces the whole sequence:

| the dependency | what it fixes in place |
|---|---|
| a sale needs the pre-emption cleared | [[preemption waiver letter]] precedes [[share sale entry]] |
| a transfer needs the meeting that approves it | [[gms director appointment]] precedes [[share sale entry]] |
| a receipt records a payment already made | [[entry payment side letter]] follows [[share sale entry]] |
| an annex needs its principal deed | the addendum follows [[perjanjian sewa fedorov]] |
| land is leased by whoever already holds the office | pack 2 follows pack 1 |

Pack 1 closes as one act: every instrument in it is signed and dated the
same day for simultaneous exchange, under [[gms director appointment]]
[§4](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/04-gms-director-appointment/gms-director-appointment#4-closing-single-condition-package).
The order below is the order of signature within that single closing, and
the closing itself is indivisible — the rule stated in [[dzin]] holds:
purchase completes if and only if the appointment takes effect.

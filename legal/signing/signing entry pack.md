## the closing rule

Purchase and appointment are a single condition package. No completed
sale means no appointment takes effect; no appointment means the sale
does not complete. Neither side may demand one without the other —
[[gms director appointment]]
[§4](/cyber-valley/cve/legal/04-gms-director-appointment/gms-director-appointment#4-closing-single-condition-package).

## form

One notarial deed — [[share sale entry]], executed before the notary.
The remaining six are private instruments (*di bawah tangan*), valid
between the parties on signature under [[kuhperdata|KUHPerdata]] Pasal
1338. Bahasa Indonesia prevails on any executed deed, per
[[uu 24-2009 bahasa|UU 24/2009]].

## what is still open

as blank rules for handwriting at the table: the passport and address of
Starodubtsev, and the address of Voinova. No document in the pack carries
them, so they are entered by hand or supplied before printing.

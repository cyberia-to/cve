## the line between this and a signed pack

An instrument reaches a signing pack when a specific person, a specific
plot or a specific sum is written into it and every open bracket against
it is closed. [[hak sewa deed]] and [[land rights agreement]] are built to
stay general, so they stay here. [[perjanjian sewa fedorov 2]] will move
to a pack of its own once the survey of sinwood-31 gives it an area.

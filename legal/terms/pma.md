# PMA — Penanaman Modal Asing / Foreign Investment Company

An Indonesian limited liability company with foreign shareholding. Formally PT PMA.

## Where it appears

PT. Cyber Valley Estate is a PMA — see [[articles of association]] Article 1.

## Why it matters

PMA status governs which business activities are open to the company ([[kbli]] codes on the Investment Priority List) and who may hold shares — [[articles of association]] Article 5(2).

# LKPM — Laporan Kegiatan Penanaman Modal

The investment activity report every PT [[pma|PMA]] files quarterly with BKPM through the OSS system: realised investment, employment, and progress against the licensed plan.

## Where it appears

[[director disclosure indemnity agreement]] Appendix C item 10 — the Company's LKPM status is unconfirmed.

## Why it matters

Missed LKPM filings are the ordinary route to administrative sanctions for a PMA: warning, suspension of the [[nib|NIB]], and in the end revocation of the business licence. The obligation sits on the Director personally as the Company's representative.

# SPV — Special Purpose Vehicle

A company formed for one project or one holding, separate from the main operating entity.

## Where it appears

[[president director]] §5 (the President Director may not mortgage Company or controlled-SPV real estate).

## Why it matters

Used so that project risk does not reach the land-holding company.

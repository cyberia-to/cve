# NIB — Nomor Induk Berusaha / Business Identification Number

The single business identity number issued through the OSS system, carrying the company's licensed activities.

## Where it appears

[[land rights agreement]] §9A.1 (the licence warranty), Annex A (the KBLI × location table).

## Why it matters

The NIB is where each [[kbli|KBLI]] code is tied to a specific business address — the known discrepancy on 68111 lives here.

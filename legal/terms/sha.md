# SHA — Shareholders' Agreement / Perjanjian Pemegang Saham

A private contract between the shareholders personally, sitting alongside the Articles of Association.

## Where it appears

[[shareholders agreement]] — the instrument itself.

## Why it matters

The Articles bind the company towards the world; the SHA binds the people to each other. Where they conflict on anything touching third parties, the Articles prevail ([[uu 40-2007 perseroan terbatas|UU 40/2007]]).

# SHM — Sertifikat Hak Milik

Certificate of freehold title, the strongest land right in Indonesian law and the only one that is perpetual. Held by Indonesian citizens only; a company cannot hold it, and a PT [[pma|PMA]] must convert an SHM parcel into [[hgb|HGB]] on acquisition.

## Where it appears

[[director disclosure indemnity agreement]] Appendix B lists the certificate for each parcel — several are still SHM in an individual seller's name rather than converted to HGB in the Company's name. [[land rights agreement]] uses SHM as the endpoint of the Indonesian-holder upgrade track.

## Why it matters

An SHM in a seller's name means the land is not the Company's, whatever was paid. Conversion to HGB at [[bpn|BPN]] through a [[ppat|PPAT]] is the act that transfers ownership; the receipt and the [[ajb|AJB]] alone do not.

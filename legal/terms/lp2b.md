# LP2B — Lahan Pertanian Pangan Berkelanjutan / Protected Sustainable Food Farmland

A designation locking agricultural land to food production and blocking conversion.

## Where it appears

[[land-rights-agreement]] 9B.1; [[legal/notes/verification-report]] §2.

## Why it matters

In a *Kawasan Pertanian* zone this stops being theoretical: it must be checked parcel by parcel before subdivision.

# SLF — Sertifikat Laik Fungsi / Certificate of Fitness for Occupancy

The permit confirming a completed building may lawfully be occupied. Issued after construction, on inspection.

## Where it appears

[[land rights agreement]] §5A.7, §7.4.2 (the Design Envelope lock runs to SLF plus 12 months), §2.5.5 (transfer fee reduction on SLF).

## Why it matters

No occupation or use of any building is lawful before SLF. It is also the moment several protections in the deed switch state.

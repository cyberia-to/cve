# PBB — Pajak Bumi dan Bangunan / Land and Building Tax

The annual property tax on land and buildings.

## Where it appears

[[land rights agreement]] 9B.1 (warranty that it is paid), §12 (allocation of cost).

## Why it matters

Arrears attach to the land, which is why the title warranty covers them explicitly.

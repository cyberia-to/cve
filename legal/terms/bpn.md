# BPN — Badan Pertanahan Nasional / National Land Agency

The Indonesian land registry. Registers titles, issues certificates, conducts cadastral surveys.

## Where it appears

[[land rights agreement]] §2.2 (hak sewa is not registered at BPN), 9B.2 (the non-encumbrance search).

## Why it matters

The fact that a lease does **not** appear at BPN is the central weakness the whole holder-protection regime in §9B is built to compensate for.

# PPJB — Perjanjian Pengikatan Jual Beli / Binding Sale and Purchase Agreement

A preliminary sale agreement used before a title can be transferred.

## Where it appears

[[land rights agreement]] 9B.1 (warranty that the plot is not already promised under a PPJB).

## Why it matters

A plot promised under an undisclosed PPJB is the classic double-sale risk — hence the express warranty.

# RTRW — Rencana Tata Ruang Wilayah / Regional Spatial Plan

The regency-level spatial plan classifying land by permitted use.

## Where it appears

[[land rights agreement]] Annex A; [[legal/notes/verification-report]] §2.

## Why it matters

The Estate's land is classified *Kawasan Pertanian* (agricultural) and adjoins *Kawasan Lindung* (protected). That classification is why [[pkkpr|PKKPR]] matters so much.

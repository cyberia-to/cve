# PBG — Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung / Building Approval

The building permit, successor to IMB. Issued to the holder of the land title, not to the occupant.

## Where it appears

[[land rights agreement]] §5.2, §5A (construction control).

## Why it matters

Because it follows the title, for a foreign holder on a lease the applicant is the Landowner — which is why the deed allocates the cost to the Holder and the indemnity with it.

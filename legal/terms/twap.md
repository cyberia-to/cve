# TWAP — Time-Weighted Average Price

An average price over a period rather than at a moment — in the [[century index]], a trailing 365-day average of daily fixes.

## Where it appears

[[century index]] §2 and §4.

## Why it matters

Used so that no single day's price can move an obligation.

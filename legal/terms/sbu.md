# SBU — Sertifikat Badan Usaha / Business Entity Certificate

The qualification certificate a construction contractor must hold under UU 2/2017.

## Where it appears

[[land rights agreement]] §5A.2.

## Why it matters

The Holder may engage only a contractor holding a valid SBU with the matching [[kbli|KBLI]] — the client is legally obliged to check.

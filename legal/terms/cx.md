# CX — Century Index — see [[century index]]

The ticker of the century index: a fixed basket of eight world assets used as a unit of account for long-duration obligations.

## Where it appears

[[land rights agreement]] §2.3, §2.4, Annex E.

## Why it matters

Whoever owes the index owes quantities, not a number. Used for 25-year lease streams; deliberately **not** used for short-dated obligations, where its volatility would work against both parties.

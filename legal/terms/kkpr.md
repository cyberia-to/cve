# KKPR — Kesesuaian Kegiatan Pemanfaatan Ruang / Spatial Use Conformity

Confirmation that an intended use conforms to the spatial plan for that location.

## Where it appears

[[land rights agreement]] §0 (condition precedent to offering a plot), 9B.1.

## Why it matters

Its detailed form is [[pkkpr|PKKPR]]. Without it, a plot cannot lawfully be built on whatever the lease says.

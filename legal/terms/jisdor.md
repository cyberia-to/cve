# JISDOR — Jakarta Interbank Spot Dollar Rate

Bank Indonesia's official daily USD/IDR reference rate.

## Where it appears

[[shareholders agreement]] Article 4, [[land-rights-agreement]] §2.3 and §12, [[century index]] thesis T6.

## Why it matters

Named explicitly in every payment clause so that conversion is not left to a bank's own rate. Source: [Bank Indonesia](https://www.bi.go.id/en/statistik/informasi-kurs/jisdor/default.aspx). Required because [[uu 7-2011 mata uang|UU 7/2011]] obliges settlement in rupiah.

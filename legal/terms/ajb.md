# AJB — Akta Jual Beli / Deed of Sale and Purchase

The notarial deed transferring land rights before a [[ppat|PPAT]].

## Where it appears

[[land-rights-agreement]] §4.1 and §4.4.

## Why it matters

Covenants that must survive a conversion to [[hak-milik|Hak Milik]] have to be recorded in the AJB — they cannot be imposed afterwards.

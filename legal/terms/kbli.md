# KBLI — Klasifikasi Baku Lapangan Usaha Indonesia / Indonesian Standard Business Classification

The code system defining what business a company may lawfully carry on. Each code is licensed for a stated location.

## Where it appears

[[articles of association]] Article 3 lists the Company's eight codes; [[land-rights-agreement]] §9A.1 warrants them.

## Why it matters

The split between land-time and stay in §2.6 exists because codes 55199 and 55192 belong to the Company and cannot be held by a foreign individual.

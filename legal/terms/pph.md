# PPh — Pajak Penghasilan / Income Tax

Indonesian income tax, including withholding on lease payments and on transfers of title.

## Where it appears

[[land-rights-agreement]] §2.3 and §12; [[articles of association]] Article 12(6) (the Director's responsibility).

## Why it matters

Who withholds and who bears it is allocated expressly in §12 so it is not argued after the fact.

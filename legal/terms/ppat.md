# PPAT — Pejabat Pembuat Akta Tanah / Land Deed Official

A notary authorised to draw up deeds transferring land rights.

## Where it appears

[[land rights agreement]] §0 (notarial form), 9B.2 (pengecekan sertifikat on the day of signing).

## Why it matters

Land instruments executed privately rather than before a PPAT are weak in practice and unusable for any later BPN procedure.

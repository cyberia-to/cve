# Annex K — Office Practice

Annexed to [[hak sewa template]]; required by its 12.1.13 before the first Hak Pakai Notice is served. Written answers of the Kantor Pertanahan of Buleleng and of counsel, obtained by the Landowner at its cost, annexed as received, and the date of each answer recorded. Until this annex carries an answer to every row, Part 12.1 of the template is drafted against assumed practice and no Notice is served.

## Effect

Where an answer differs from the route in [[hak sewa template]] 12.1.1, the office's order of the same steps governs and the steps themselves stand. Where an answer closes a row of [[open questions]], that row is marked answered with a pointer here. Answers age: the Landowner refreshes K5 annually and every row before a Notice is served more than 24 months after the answer was given.

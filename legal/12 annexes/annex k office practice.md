# Annex K — Office Practice

Annexed to [[hak sewa template]] under 12.1.13. The written answers of the Kantor Pertanahan of Buleleng and of counsel, obtained by the Landowner at its cost before the first Hak Pakai Notice, each with its date.

## Form

| # | question | answered by | answer, date |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | the order of *pemecahan sertifikat*, *pelepasan hak* and the grant of Hak Pakai the office requires for a foreign natural person over a parcel split from a PT PMA HGB | Kantor Pertanahan | |
| K2 | the stage of construction at which the office accepts the application: PBG, SLF, or a completed and valued house | Kantor Pertanahan | |
| K3 | the documents the office requires from a PT PMA as registered holder, and from the applicant | Kantor Pertanahan | |
| K4 | the treatment of HGB 00048/Gesing under RTRW, [[lp2b|LP2B]] and *sempadan* for this purpose | Kantor Pertanahan | |
| K5 | the current minimum value, maximum area and parcel limit for a foreign holder in the Province of Bali | counsel, against JDIH | |
| K6 | the instrument through which the Holder signs under 12.1.8 where the Landowner is late | notary and counsel | |
| K7 | the evidence of *rumah tinggal* the office accepts for Hak Milik under 12.2 | Kantor Pertanahan | |
| K8 | the covenant and easement mechanism the PPAT records in the acquisition deed under 12.1.14 and 12.3 | notary and PPAT | |

## Effect

Where an answer differs from the route in [[hak sewa template]] 12.1.1, the office's order of the same steps governs and the steps themselves stand. The Landowner refreshes K5 each year and, before serving a Notice, every answer older than 24 months.

# Annex D — Community Rules

Annexed to [[hak sewa template]]; reasoning in [[land rights agreement]]. Binding on all Holders under
[[annex c design code|Annex C]] §5, which names the estate's policy pages
as the pollution and resource standards a Holder's construction and
occupation must meet.

This annex does not restate those pages — one body of rules, stated once,
on the policy side of the graph. The policy pages are the standard,
**incorporated by reference** and version-locked at signing: the version
that binds a Holder is the page as it stood on the signing date,
identified by commit hash and date on the same mechanism [[annex j offer
of record|Annex J]] uses for the offer. Amendments reach a Holder only
through [[hak sewa template]] 10.5, subject to the Community Council veto under 11.2.

## 2. Adat record

[[hak sewa template]] 17.2 binds the Holder to the customary arrangements affecting the Estate. The Landowner records them here per district before the first signing in that district, and updates the record with the *desa adat*:

| item | record |
|---|---|
| *desa adat* and *banjar* | name; the *awig-awig* provisions touching land use, building and ceremony, by article |
| *subak* | any subak channel or right crossing the district; maintenance duty; water-sharing rule |
| ceremonial access | processional routes and temple approaches; days on which they are used |
| customary contributions | the resident's expected contribution, in kind or in money, and its cadence |

An item recorded here is disclosed under 13.3 and initialled with Annex C. An item arising later reaches the Holder through 10.5 as an act of adat under 10.6.3.

## 3. Enforcement and relation to the deed

A breach of a policy standard is a breach of [[annex c design code|Annex
C]] under the Design Code procedure it sets, one cause of action rather
than two. First instance — notice and mandatory remediation at the
Holder's cost within the cure period of [[hak sewa template]] 16.1.
Second instance — the same standard breached again within 12 months of
the first remediation is a material default on the second notice under
16.1, with the consequences of Part 16. Where a specific Part of the
template names a consequence for a category of breach, that Part's
mechanism applies: the policy pages state the standard, the deed states
what happens when it is broken.

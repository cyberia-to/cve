# annexes

Eleven schedules to [[hak sewa template]]. Each states what it is, whether it is completed per deal or stands for the estate, and which Part of the template reaches it.

| annex | page | per deal or standing | reached from |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | [[annex a estate registry|estate registry — titles, terms, KBLI by address]] | standing, dated | recitals, 0.4, 14.1, 15.1 |
| B | [[annex b plot boundary|plot boundary — patok, UTM, peta bidang]] | per deal | 1, 6, 9.1 |
| C | [[annex c design code|design code — footprint, light balance, biodiversity, built form]] | standing, version-locked | Part 10 |
| D | [[annex d community rules|community rules — six policy pages, adat record]] | standing, version-locked | 4.4, 16.1, 17.2 |
| E | [[annex e century index|century index — quantities, dual floor, worked invoice]] | per deal figures on a standing machine | 2.4, 3.4, 3.5, 11.4 |
| F | [[annex f utilities access mobility|utilities, access, mobility — car-free core, logistics, waste]] | standing, district rows per deal | 9.1, 10.6, 11.5 |
| G | [[annex g permitted use|permitted use — instruments, zones, net-exporter, referral threshold]] | standing | 4.5, 5, 10.6 |
| H | [[annex h handover condition|handover condition — baseline at BAST Lahan]] | per deal | 8.6, 16.5 |
| I | [[annex i due diligence pack|due diligence pack — title, search, PKKPR]] | per deal, at signing | 9.1, 15.1.1 |
| J | [[annex j offer of record|offer of record — product page by commit hash]] | per deal | 3.0 |
| K | [[annex k office practice|office practice — Kantor Pertanahan Buleleng and counsel answers]] | standing, before the first Hak Pakai Notice | 12.1.13 |

The century index is the annex signed instruments already work through: it prices the renewal term under Pasal 8 of [[perjanjian sewa fedorov|the Fedorov lease]] and denominates indexed consideration under [[hak sewa deed]] §3.3.

One lettering collision worth knowing at a signing table: Annex G of [[perjanjian sewa fedorov|the Fedorov lease]] is [[leasehold agreement]], and a deed's own lettering governs.

# annexes

- [[annex e century index|E · century index — weights, fixes, floor, worked invoice]]

The century index is the annex signed instruments already work through: it
prices the renewal term under Pasal 8 of [[perjanjian sewa fedorov|the
Fedorov lease]] and denominates indexed consideration under [[hak sewa
deed]] §3.3.

The other nine annexes — A–D and F–J — are working schedules of
[[land rights agreement]] and stay out of this index until they are
settled. They live in this folder and are reached from the framework's own
text.

One lettering collision worth knowing at a signing table: Annex G of
[[perjanjian sewa fedorov|the Fedorov lease]] is [[leasehold agreement]],
not this pack's permitted-use matrix — a deed's own lettering governs.

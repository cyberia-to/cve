# Annex C — Design Code

Annexed to [[hak sewa template]] under its Part 10; reasoning in [[land rights agreement]]. Binding on all Holders, all tracks, all zones, and on any assignee or successor.

This annex and [[cyber-valley/policies/zoning system|land usage policy]] state one body of rules in two registers. The policy page holds the reasoning and the working numbers as the estate maintains them — the informal representation, open to anyone reading the graph. This annex holds the same numbers in the form that binds a Holder under the deed — the formal representation, initialled and version-locked at signing under [[hak sewa template]] 10.6. Read either one and you are reading the same rules.

Where a policy page is amended, this annex is amended with it under [[hak sewa template]] 10.5, subject to the veto in 11.2. Where a Bali provincial or Buleleng regulation is stricter, that regulation prevails.

## C1. Measurement

C1.1 Ratios in C2 and C3 are measured per Plot, on the area shown in Annex B (Plot Boundary).

C1.2 Unused footprint entitlement is transferable to another Plot in the same district through the Register under [[hak sewa template]] 10.4, against a settlement fee, and only where the district balance in C7 is maintained. Transfers open from Wave 2.

C1.3 The Holder's compliance is measured at [[slf|SLF]] and at each periodic SLF renewal, and on any transfer under [[hak sewa template]] Part 4.

C1.4 A roof counts in C3 by its surface: a photovoltaic or collector roof is a light absorber, a glazed roof a transparent surface, a planted roof the vegetation class it carries. A basement counts as a floor for the C2 multiplier only where it rises above natural grade.

## C2. Construction area

Informal counterpart: construction area in [[cyber-valley/policies/zoning system|land usage policy]].

| parameter | value |
|---|---|
| maximum ground footprint | 10% of the Plot |
| multiplier | ×2 where two floors are used — 20% of effective floor area on the same footprint |
| purpose | protect soil, enable water infiltration, preserve living ecosystems |

On a 500 m² Plot this is 50 m² of footprint and up to 100 m² of floor area. ⚠ This is the single most consequential number in the deed for the Holder and is disclosed before signing, in figures, for the actual Plot.

## C3. Light balance

Informal counterpart: light balance in [[cyber-valley/policies/zoning system|land usage policy]].

100% of the solar energy falling on the Plot is allocated across these receivers:

| receiver | minimum | maximum | reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| light absorbers — photovoltaics, heat collectors | 1% | 10% | energy security / biome balance |
| water bodies | 1% | 10% | water security / biome balance |
| transparent surfaces — greenhouses | 2% | 10% | food security / biome balance |
| lawn or cover | — | 5% | biome balance |
| herb or shrub | 30% | — | biome balance |
| dwarf or canopy | 40% | — | biome balance |

## C4. Biodiversity

Informal counterpart: biodiversity in [[cyber-valley/policies/zoning system|land usage policy]].

Minimum 20 plant species per hectare, pro rata for smaller Plots and in every case at least 10 species on a Plot, counted at SLF and at each renewal. Species on the estate's invasive list, published in the Register, count for nothing.

## C5. Pollution limits

Informal counterpart: pollution in [[cyber-valley/policies/zoning system|land usage policy]], which routes each limit to its own policy page.

Pollution is a resource in the wrong place. Each type is governed by its
policy page — [[sound policy]], [[water policy]], [[light policy]],
[[air policy]], [[soil policy]], [[carbon policy]] — and compliance is a
condition of land use. The limits are not restated here: the pages are
incorporated by reference and version-locked at signing through
[[annex d community rules|Annex D (Community Rules)]], which also carries the two-strike
enforcement and the routing into [[hak sewa deed]].

## C6. Monitoring and publication

| duty | frequency | who pays | where published |
|---|---|---|---|
| discharge monitoring | quarterly | Holder | cyberia ledger and the Register |
| soil test per Plot | annual | Holder | cyberia ledger and the Register |
| species count | at SLF and each SLF renewal | Holder | Register |

Publication carries the Plot identifier and the result, not the Holder's identity. Failure to publish for two consecutive periods is a Design Code breach for the purposes of the transfer fee scale in [[hak sewa template]] 4.5 and, if unremedied after notice under 16.1, a default.

## C7. District balance

For each district the Landowner maintains and publishes in the Register the aggregate of C2 and C3 across all Plots: total district area, footprint entitlement granted, footprint transferred in and out, and the C3 receiver shares. A transfer under C1.2 is entered only where, after it, district footprint stays at or below 10% of district area and every C3 minimum still holds district-wide. ⚠ The per-district area figures enter once the Z1 grain is settled.

## C9. Overlays that bind regardless

Provincial building-height limits, architectural requirements, slope and ravine setbacks, water-catchment restrictions, *sempadan sungai*, subak and *jalan desa*. These bind whatever this annex says.

## C10. Grandfathering

Improvements completed in compliance with the version of this annex in force at their SLF remain compliant. An amendment worsening their position requires the veto threshold in [[hak sewa template]] 11.2.
